How the numbers are measured

Distance
GPS trail, fixes ≤ 10 min apart, ignoring jitter under 25 m and hops above 5 km
Fuel
Fills recorded on Fuel & efficiency; km/litre per vehicle
Cost / km
Fuel + service + other expenses over GPS distance
Speeding
Fixes above the limit in Fleet settings (currently 80 km/h)
Utilisation
Share of the last 30 days' fixes above the moving threshold
Driver score
Trips, distance and late starts from the transport trips; speeding only from fixes inside a recorded trip window
Inspections
A car inspected inside the window set in Fleet settings; a blank history counts as overdue
Trips vs GPS
Billed odometer distance compared with the trail during the trip window; only completed trips with an odometer and both timestamps are checked
Service forecast
Day intervals as recorded; kilometre intervals projected from the vehicle's own 30-day GPS distance
Papers
The transport desk's warning window, applied to insurance, permit, fitness and PUC
Fills to check
Consumption under 60% of the vehicle's own median, a frozen odometer, or an odometer that disagrees with the GPS trail by over 25%
Off-trip
At least 1 km driven today with no trip scheduled for the vehicle
