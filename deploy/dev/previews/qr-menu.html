What the guest sees

  1. The dish tile shows how many sides it carries (3 sides), so nobody taps expecting a plain plate.
  2. ADD opens the side sheet first — tick one or more, or continue with none.
  3. A side with a rate shows +GH₵ 12.50; a side with no rate in the menu master shows included and adds nothing.
  4. If the dish also has options (spice level, doneness), the sheet moves on to them with the chosen sides already ticked.
  5. The order reaches the POS as pending — no bill and no KOT until staff accept it, and the sides print with the line.
Server side: the sheet posts items[n][variants][]; the controller validates each id against this property, runs it through the same side-dish snapshot the terminal uses, and merges it into the stored add-on list with type: side_dish.
