How to read this statement
  • Only vehicles with something recorded in October 2026 appear — a quiet car is left off rather than printed as a row of zeroes.
  • Distance is the GPS trail from the paired trackers, measured fix to fix and ignoring hops under 25 m or over 5 km. No trail means the car reported no fixes at all that month; cost per km is then blank, because dividing by zero would invent a number.
  • Fleet cost per km covers only the cars that were measurable (3 of 4 this month).
  • Figures are the expenses recorded against each vehicle. They are not a purchase ledger and not a tax invoice — reconcile against the garage and filling-station receipts before posting to accounts.
