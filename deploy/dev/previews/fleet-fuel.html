Two consecutive fixes at most 10 minutes apart, both reporting the ignition on, with the later one below 5 km/h, count as standing time for that interval. A car parked overnight with the ignition off contributes nothing, and a gap longer than 10 minutes is skipped rather than credited to idling. The litre figure is an assumption priced from Fleet settings — the minutes and the rankings are measured.
