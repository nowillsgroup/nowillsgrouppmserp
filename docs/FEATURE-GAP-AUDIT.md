**Audit method:** every module's routes/controllers/services/views read from source; the gap checklist below enumerates every area where the old PMS/ERP behaved differently or did more; package requirements verified against `composer.json` and runtime checks — the application ships no npm build, so there is no root `package.json`; the only one in the repository is `deploy/dev/package.json`, which belongs to the dev runtime, and the front end is plain CSS and JavaScript under `public/`.

> **How this document is kept true.** `deploy/dev/audit/gap_audit_check.py` diffs every claim below against the running
> system, in both directions: each artifact named in backticks must resolve to a class, a method, a command, a route, a
> file, a driver id, a setting or a column; each of the twelve classes and one command named as *not* existing must
> still be absent; each closed claim must have its service, driver, route, simulator and specification behind it; and
> each still-open claim must still be open — so the moment one of them ships, the check fails until this document is
> updated. That last direction is the one that matters, because a gap audit only ever rots one way: the capability
> ships and the document keeps saying it is missing, which is exactly what had happened to four of the items below.
> Facts come from `deploy/dev/audit/gap_facts.php` and the route list, and either is regenerated when `config/pms.php`
> is newer than the dump, so a stale dump can never report the old system as the live one.

## Confirmed delivery baseline

Fully delivered and working (code + UI + migrations + tests where listed):

- **Core PMS:** multi-property PMS core — reservations, folios, cashier, POS, AR, night audit, housekeeping, interfaces.
- **Business intelligence:** KPI service + dashboards — occupancy/ADR/RevPAR/ALOS, forecast vs budget, pace and lead-time analysis, source/country segments, revenue by outlet, top-guest and cancellation-no-show analytics, 30-day sparkline.
- **Add-on billing:** service charge 10% + auto-10% package toggle, renewable-energy levy 2% and VAT-exempt package, guest amenity (DND), turndown and romantic-setup packages.
- **Pro-forma invoice / quotation:** ✅ delivered as a document state rather than a service — there is no `ProformaService`. `resources/views/folios/invoice.blade.php` titles the same folio PRO-FORMA BILL before it is issued, VAT INVOICE (Ghana) or INVOICE once it is, and CREDIT NOTE for a credit note; quotations and banqueting proposals are `SalesService` and `BanquetSalesService`.
- **Allotments:** free-sale/allotment inventory for companies and channels, plus manual allotment release on the tape chart.
- **Rate rules + packages + season calendar:** ✅ delivered, though none of the three services this line named exists (`RateRuleService`, `RatePackageService`, `SeasonService`). `RateService` owns restrictions — every override covering a date combines, so any stop-sell closes it and the highest minimum stay applies — the stay quote and the rate calendar grid, whose cells carry the season, weekend, fixed and dynamic source of each date; `RateOverride` and `RatePlan` hold the rules; `PackageService` with `Package`, `PackageEntitlement` and `PackageRedemption` holds packages; `RateContract` and `RateContractRate` hold corporate and seasonal contract rates.
- **Guest portal:** reviews, documents, payments and messages on the tokenised stay portal — the routes are `stay/{token}` with `stay/{token}/bill`, `stay/{token}/chat`, `stay/{token}/checkout` and `stay/{token}/dining` under it, not the `guest/*` prefix this line used to claim. Staff-side guest requests are `guest-requests`.
- **Digital registration cards:** ✅ delivered, though not by the two artifacts this line used to name — there is no `SignatureService` and no `signaturepad` npm package (the application has no npm build at all). The self-service lobby kiosk (v7.2) is the registration form: `KioskController` drives the visit and `KioskService` owns each step, the guest signs on a canvas pad drawn by hand in `public/js/kiosk.js`, and `KioskService::sign()` validates the PNG data URL it posts — a real PNG, under 2000×1000, at least 500 bytes of ink — before storing it at `grc/{hotel}/{stay}-{random}.png` on the local disk and stamping `grc_signature_path` and `grc_signed_at` on the `reservation_rooms` row, replacing any earlier signature. Inbound webhooks are configured per hotel (the review push at `POST /api/reviews/{token}` and the payment webhook at `POST /webhooks/pay/{provider}/{code}` both carry a 40-character per-hotel token).
- **Bus/flight manifests:** manifest management + template generation.
- **Cost control, yield/revenue, data manager, banqueting, sales & marketing.**
- **Bedroom bookings** (per-bed inventory).
- **Call accounting** (`CallAccountingService`, `CallRecord`, `CallTariff`, `CallController`, `calls/index.blade.php`) — ACDR/SMDR parsing, extension mapping, tariff rating (VAT + service charge), auto-posting to guest ledgers or the in-house list, duplicate and zero-duration protection, daily statistics and reports. **Capture path: CSV import + `api/pbx/{token}` HTTP push + live SMDR capture (new — see Completed).**

## What's missing — gaps versus the previous product (priority-ranked)

1. **P1 — OTA / channel-manager two-way adapters.** *Largely closed — see the P1 status list below.* The `ChannelDriver` contract (`test`, `pushAri`, `pullBookings`, `cancelBooking`), the driver registry, the ARI dirty-tracking/pricing/chunking pipeline and the inbound mapping pipeline all ship, with four real adapters behind them: Booking.com EPS, Expedia Rapid (HMAC-SHA512 signing), SiteMinder (OTA XML, the channel-manager adapter that fronts Booking.com/Expedia/Agoda/Airbnb) and the generic mirror feed. Outbound cancellation is wired into every PMS cancel path (v7.42). **Foreign-currency OTA bookings: closed v7.50.** A booking whose currency is not the hotel currency is now converted at the forex board (`ForexService::boardRate()`), on a basis and staleness window the hotel sets under `Foreign Guests › Settings › Forex desk`; the original currency, amount, rate and board date are kept on the reservation, and a currency with no rate on the board is held in the inbox as `no_fx_rate` (reprocessable once a rate is set) rather than rejected. Switching the setting off restores the old rejection. See `docs/CHANNEL-ADAPTER.md` › Foreign-currency bookings. **Next:** the remaining named sources (STAAH / RateGain / SynXis / Cubilis / DJUBO / Hotelbeds / Airbnb direct) as incremental drivers. See `docs/CHANNEL-ADAPTER.md`, `docs/SITEMINDER.md`, `docs/BOOKING-EPS.md`, `docs/EXPEDIA-RAPID.md`.
2. **P2 — Social media (Facebook page posting, WhatsApp Cloud API) and review-platform integration.** ✅ **Closed (v7.39)** — the checklist below said so while this summary still said the opposite. `SocialPublishService` posts to the Facebook Page Graph API (`POST {graph}/v20.0/{page_id}/feed`) and WhatsApp Cloud (`POST {graph}/v20.0/{phone_id}/messages`), with the access tokens held encrypted in the `social` secret, and pulls platform review feeds into `ReviewService` and the review inbox; inbound review pushes arrive at `POST /api/reviews/{token}`; `deploy/dev/social-sim.mjs` stands in for Meta so the whole path is testable offline (`docs/SOCIAL-REVIEWS.md`). **Remaining:** Instagram, X and LinkedIn channels are recorded-only — the post is marked published with its URL rather than delivered, because those platforms have no equivalent publish API for a hotel page.
3. **P2 — Bank/MoMo payment gateway integrations.** ✅ **Closed** — and this line named a class that has never existed (`PaystackService`; the gateway is `PaystackGateway`). Bank statements import from CSV, Excel xlsx, SWIFT MT940/MT942 and ISO 20022 camt.052/camt.053 through `BankRecService::readFile()`, all four reducing to one parse path with running-balance cross-checks (v7.48, `docs/BANK-STATEMENT-FORMATS.md`). Request-to-pay mobile money on MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money collects, polls, refunds and settles onto the folio with a signed webhook and an idempotent completion (`MoMoGateway`, `OnlinePaymentService::complete()`, v7.37, `docs/MOMO-GATEWAY.md`, `deploy/dev/momo-sim.mjs`). `PaystackGateway`, `StripeGateway`, `RazorpayGateway` and `DemoGateway` sit behind the same `CollectGateway` contract, and `TreasuryService` holds the bank side.
4. **P2 — GRA e-invoicing (electronic invoicing).** *Substantially closed (v4.3 E-VAT, v4.4 restaurant bills) — "submissions are still manual" was wrong.* `EInvoiceService` submits every closed tax invoice and credit note to the GRA Virtual Sales Data Controller and stores the stamp it returns: SDC id, receipt counter, internal data, signature, timestamp and QR code, all printed on the document. There are two drivers — an offline VSDC simulator that applies the checks GRA rejects most often and signs the way the VSDC does (`deploy/dev/vsdc-sim.mjs` for the HTTP one), and the live `gra` driver, which is the `submitInvoice` endpoint this line said was missing: a signed POST to the configured VSDC URL behind `IcalService::assertSafeUrl()`. One document is submitted from `POST /vat/documents/{folio}/e-vat`, all due documents from `POST /vat/e-vat/retry`, and `pms:einvoice-retry` retries pending and retryable failures every five minutes on a 5/15/60/180/720-minute back-off, with every attempt — payload, response, duration, user — kept in `EinvoiceLog`. Restaurant bills go through `PosEvatService` on the same driver. A stamped document cannot be cancelled, so corrections are credit notes carrying `flag=REFUND` and the original invoice number.

   **Genuinely remaining, stated precisely because neither part is ours to finish alone:** (a) the GRA field names are modelled on the published E-VAT API but **unconfirmed** — the driver's own docstring says to confirm them with GRA during joint user-acceptance testing before switching a property from `sandbox` to `gra`; (b) ~~there is no per-property VAT-registration record~~ — **closed (v7.52).** `hotels` now carries `vat_registered`, `vat_registered_at`, `vat_supply_kind` and `supports_e_invoicing` (backfilled as registered wherever a `tax_number` was already on file), edited under Administration › Settings. `GstService::vatRegistration()` applies the Value Added Tax Act, 2025 (Act 1151) test to the property's own taxable turnover — the two twelve-month windows, taking whichever is larger — and reports `registered`, `must_register`, `approaching` or `below_threshold` with the reason in plain English, because the threshold depends on what the property supplies: **none for services** (a hotel, restaurant or guest house must register whatever it turns over) and **GH₵750,000 for goods**, raised from GH₵200,000. `EInvoiceService::registrationBlocker()` is the gate: an unregistered property queues nothing, its submit controls disappear, and a forced submit fails saying why, so the `gst_einvoice_enabled` switch is no longer the only thing standing between a property and the VSDC. Billing › GRA VAT › Settings shows the test. Proven by `deploy/dev/audit/vat_registration.php` (42 checks) and `deploy/dev/audit/vat_registration_flow.py` (41 checks, driven through the real forms).
5. **P3 — Offline mode (desktop).** ✅ **Closed this session:** `DesktopSyncService` multi-day cache-and-sync — pack export (7-day desk dataset + delta cursor), `desk.*` operation queue (check-in/out, charge, payment, night posting) with occurred_at back-dating and applied/duplicate/rejected results, `delta` incremental refresh, `/offline/desktop` workspace + `pms:offline:pack` command. See `docs/OFFLINE-DESKTOP.md`. *(POS draft caching and the housekeeping/guest-request queues are unchanged.)*

**Hardware-dependent (deferred):** Bluetooth/hotel-key-card-lock driver bindings. The PMS implements card issuance, encoding and revocation APIs (`KeyCardService`); no lock-vendor hardware driver is bundled. This is **not** reproducible in CI and requires vendor-specific SDKs or hardware — deferred until the specific lock vendor/SDK is chosen. `PackageManifest` and `Spatie` diagnostics cover extension/plugin detection.

*Previously fixed (see prior audits):* P1 wake-up calls (`pms:frontdesk-run`, the five-minute escalation task in `FrontDeskOpsService` over the `WakeupCall` model — the `pms:wakeup:run` this line used to name does not exist) and the P1 data-migration/cutover toolkit — **closed this session** (below).

## Gap checklist — where the old PMS/ERP outperforms the new one

### P0 — core still missing
- **Grading / guest profiles / billing reconciliation** — *none of the three classes this line named exists* (`GradingService`, `GuestBillRec`, `FolioRec`); the real artifacts are: grading is the **GTA Star Rating / Grading Certificate** register item in `ComplianceService` (licence and statutory compliance register with expiry, renewal lead time and a daily digest); guest profiles exist; billing reconciliation is `BankRecService` for the bank and MoMo side plus the folio audit trail (`ActivityLogger`, void-with-reason, night audit) for the guest side. **Still open:** the old system's richer billing-reconciliation audit trail — a document-level reconciliation of what was billed against what was collected and settled, with its own working paper — has no equivalent here.

### P1 — OTA/channel + integration surface
- **OTA / channel-manager two-way adapters** — substantially closed (v7.42): `hub`, `simulator`, `mirror`, **Booking.com EPS**, **Expedia Rapid** (HMAC-SHA512 signing) and **SiteMinder** (OTA XML — `OTA_AvailRateNotifyRQ` / `OTA_HotelResModifyNotifRS`, the channel-manager adapter that fronts Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and Airbnb at once) all ship and all pass one contract harness — 54 checks, 0 failures (`docs/CHANNEL-ADAPTER.md`, `docs/SITEMINDER.md`). The loop is now two-way in both directions: an OTA feed creates/cancels real reservations, and `ChannelService::cancelAtChannel()` cancels at the channel when the desk cancels here (post-commit, never fatal, flagged on `channel_attention` when the channel refuses). Three defects fixed on the way: `expedia` was missing from the selectable provider list so the shipped Rapid driver could not be configured at all; the endpoint/API-key fields were hidden for every type except `hub`; and the mirror adapter pulled and cancelled at `{endpoint}` instead of `{endpoint}/bookings`, 404ing on every hub-protocol feed. A foreign-currency OTA booking is converted at the forex board since v7.50 (was: rejected into the inbox). Remaining: STAAH / RateGain / SynXis / Cubilis / DJUBO / Hotelbeds / Airbnb direct are still driver-less.
- **Social media integration (Facebook page posting, WhatsApp Cloud API)** — ✅ **Closed:** `SocialPublishService` Graph/Cloud connectors (`docs/SOCIAL-REVIEWS.md`).
- **Review platform integration (Booking.com, Google, TripAdvisor review inbox)** — ✅ **Closed:** feed pulls into the review inbox (`docs/SOCIAL-REVIEWS.md`).
- **`DesktopSyncService` + offline mode** — ✅ **Closed this session:** multi-day pack + operation sync + delta refresh (`docs/OFFLINE-DESKTOP.md`).

### P2 — revenue capture / integrations
- **Bank account statement feeds** — ✅ **Closed:** `BankRecService::import/parse` imports bank + MoMo CSVs with running-balance checks; fee booking in one action.
- **Mobile-money (MTN MoMo / Telecel Cash / AirtelTigo Money) payments** — ✅ **Closed:** MoMo pay-in + webhook simulation and settlement through bank reconciliation.
- **PABX live call capture** — ✅ **Closed this session:** `pms:pbx:listen` live SMDR capture (`connect` + `listen` modes) — `TcpPbxDriver` (command link + SMDR stream), `pms:pbx:listen` streaming importer, Interfaces › PABX driver selector (demo/offline/tcp) + SMDR field-order templates + link-test control, sim fixture `deploy/dev/pbx-sim.mjs`, `docs/PBX-ADAPTER.md` protocol spec. *(HTTP push `api/pbx/{token}` + CSV import existed; "live TCP" is now real — no vendor SDK required for SMDR capture.)*
- **Supplier catalog + purchase order + delivery note + stock + bar/restaurant cost + invoice register** — mostly present, though not under the names this line gave (`SupplierService` and `InventoryService` do not exist): `ProcurementService` owns suppliers, RFQs (`RfqSupplier`), purchase orders (`PurchaseOrder` / `PurchaseOrderItem`) and receipts, `StockTakeService` and `StockMovement` own stock, `InventoryItem` is the catalog, `PmService` and `FbCostService` own bar and restaurant cost. Multi-currency supplier invoicing ✅ **Closed (v7.53):** a bill can be entered in the currency the supplier billed in and is converted at the forex board — `ApService::convertLines()`, `ApService::fxNote()`, `ApService::fxExposure()`, `ForexService::apBoard()`, `ForexService::apPolicy()`, `ForexService::apConvert()` and `SupplierInvoice::fxOutstanding()` — on seven new `supplier_invoices` columns (`currency`, `fx_subtotal`, `fx_tax`, `fx_total`, `fx_rate`, `fx_date`, `fx_basis`), with the exposure card on AP Ageing and the policy under Foreign Guests › Settings (`docs/MULTI-CURRENCY.md`). Purchase-order currency stays open: a bill matched to a goods receipt is valued at the receipt, which is in the property's currency, and is refused in any other.
- **Ticketing / OTA review webhook** — cases (ticketing) exist (`CaseService`); OTA review webhook ✅ **Closed:** `POST /api/reviews/{token}` per-hotel push endpoint feeding the review inbox (`docs/SOCIAL-REVIEWS.md`).
- **Rental car / bus / flight / activity booking** — car rental now a first-class module (fleet, bookings, pick-up/return desk with fuel/mileage/late adjustments, folio billing); bus/flight/activity remain template-driven in transport.

### P3 — hardware / offline
- **Room telephone PABX, keycard, mini-bar, serial captures** — ⚠️ **Partially covered:** call accounting + PABX (`TcpPbxDriver`, `pms:pbx:listen`) + key-card issue/encode/revoke (`KeyCardService`) + `InterfaceService` exist; the mini-bar is the `MinibarItem` model and its posting path rather than a `MiniBarService`, which does not exist. **Still open:** the key-card **hardware driver** is not bundled (needs a vendor SDK). *(Lock-card hardware deferred — vendor-specific.)*
